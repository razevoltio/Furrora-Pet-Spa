Questions, answered

Can't find yours? Message us on WhatsApp.

How long does a spa visit take?

Most visits take 2 to 3 hours depending on breed, coat, and the package you choose. We will confirm timing when you book.

Is my pet safe with sensitive skin or allergies?

Yes. We use hypoallergenic, organic shampoos. Tell us about any allergies or skin conditions when you book.

Do you groom cats too?

Yes. Cats get a quiet, patient session with gentle handling and no rushing.

My puppy has never been groomed. Is that okay?

Absolutely. Our Puppy's First Spa Day is designed for exactly this.
